Adwam privacy policy

What stays in app storage

Adwam calculates calendar reminders and prayer times on your phone. Your fasting trackers, ticks and make-up count are stored in the app. Android may include them in your system backup or device transfer if you enable those settings. Adwam excludes its sign-in credentials from Android backups.

You can choose a city yourself or let Adwam use Android's approximate location to pick the nearest city. You can change the city or revoke the location permission at any time.

Weather and date corrections

Adwam creates an installation identifier automatically. You can use the app without creating an account. The reminder server stores the identifier, app and content versions, country, time zone, Hijri calculation settings, and notification settings needed to deliver reminders. It also stores a push token when available.

While weather reminders are enabled, the app sends a weather area rounded to a tenth of a degree of latitude and longitude. The server requests forecasts for that area from Open-Meteo. Adwam uses Supabase for its server and Expo and Firebase to deliver Android pushes.

Crash reports

Crash reporting is on at first. You can turn it off in Settings → Privacy. Reports sent to Sentry include software versions, stack frames and counts from reminder scheduling. Adwam filters entered text, location, install identifiers and tracker data out of these app reports.

Usage statistics

Adwam sends usage statistics to PostHog in the EU if you choose Share. These include screen views and selected reminder interactions, the app and content versions, platform, country and a separate random identifier. Using a Sunnah app may indicate religious beliefs. Adwam disables IP-based location enrichment, session replay and automatic touch capture.

You can withdraw permission at any time in Settings → Privacy. Turning off usage statistics drops pending events and clears the analytics identifier from the phone.

Retention and your choices

Choose Settings → Privacy → Delete my data from the server to remove the saved reminder settings and push token for this installation. The app signs out and clears its server cache. You keep your trackers. Adwam removes saved reminder records after 120 days of inactivity.

Crash reports, usage statistics and provider logs have separate retention periods. You can also change Android location and notification permissions in your phone's settings.

Contact

The final notice will identify Adwam's operator and include a privacy contact for access, correction and deletion requests.
